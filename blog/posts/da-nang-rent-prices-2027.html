How Much Is Rent in Da Nang, and Why Is 2027 a Big Year?

Today's rents by area and size, and the projects that start or switch on in 2027.

How much is rent in Da Nang right now?

A one bedroom apartment costs about $690 a month and a two bedroom about $1,400. In my listings on 5 November 2026, across 96 homes, those were the medians for leases of six months or more. Half the homes cost less than these figures and half cost more.

How does rent change by area?

The beach grid of My An and the quieter streets of Son Tra are the cheapest places for a one bedroom. The riverside towers of Hai Chau and the My Khe beachfront cost the most, and Ngu Hanh Son, further south, has the most villas.

Are rents in Da Nang going up?

Yes, slowly, and sale prices are rising faster than rents. Da Nang's statistics office reported rental prices up 2.03 percent in a single month in early 2026, which it put down to steady demand and repairs after the holidays. Over a longer stretch, the Vietnam Association of Realtors puts apartment sale prices up about 68 percent since early 2019, with new apartments selling at about 83 million dong per square metre, 14 percent more than in 2024.

My own listings moved little this autumn. On 14 October 2026 the median one bedroom was $688 and the median two bedroom $1,385. On 5 November they were $688 and $1,400. Three weeks is too short to call a trend, so treat that as a snapshot.

Higher sale prices reach renters over time. An owner who paid more for a new apartment asks more for it, so the newest towers set the top of the market while older buildings stay cheaper.

Why is 2027 a big year for Da Nang?

Because several of the city's largest projects start or switch on in 2027. Most of them finish later, between 2029 and 2032, so 2027 is the year the work becomes visible.

When will the Da Nang metro open?

Not before 2032. THACO and Hyundai Rotem plan to break ground in February 2027 on a line of more than 103 km, costing about 266 trillion dong, or $10.1 billion. The first phase runs 30.2 km with 14 stations from Da Nang airport to Hoi An, and the second continues 73.6 km to Chu Lai airport. Some approvals were still pending in April 2026.

The exact route through the city has not been published. Choose a home for how you live today, not for a station that may open in six years. Expect construction on parts of the airport to Hoi An corridor from 2027, which can mean diversions and noise near the work sites.

What changes at Da Nang airport in 2027?

More room for planes in July 2027, and work on a bigger terminal from August. The airport operator is spending about 9.9 trillion dong, or $380 million, to expand Terminal 1 to 14 million passengers a year, with completion planned for January 2029. The first phase of a new apron, with six aircraft stands, is due to enter service in July 2027.

Demand is already there. Da Nang welcomed nearly 5.2 million international arrivals in the first half of 2026, up 28.7 percent on a year earlier, and new direct flights opened from Moscow and Vladivostok. More visitors means more short-stay competition for apartments near the beach, which is one reason long leases there are worth locking in. Our guide to a month on Airbnb or a lease compares the two.

Will new apartments make renting cheaper?

They will give you more choice, mostly at the top end. Savills counts about 12,300 new apartments from 19 projects reaching the market between the second half of 2025 and 2027. Research firm DKRA put the city's total apartment stock at about 16,000 units in early 2026, and nearly 60 percent of 2025's new supply was high-end.

Many buyers of new apartments rent them out, so each tower that opens adds rental listings. That adds competition between landlords in new high-end buildings, and it gives tenants more room to negotiate on furniture, parking and the length of the lease. Older mid-range buildings depend more on demand from people moving to the city for work.

Who is moving to Da Nang for work?

More finance, logistics and tech staff, as three long projects take shape. Da Nang's international financial centre opened its first office at Software Park No. 2 on 9 January 2026 and certified 12 investors within two months. The free trade zone gained its rules in May 2026 under Decree 33/2026, and by August four of its seven sites had approved projects. At Lien Chieu, Hateco and APM Terminals broke ground on a container port worth more than $1.7 billion on 25 April 2026, with operations targeted for early 2029.

None of these fills an office tower overnight. Each adds managers, engineers and consultants over several years, and many of them rent rather than buy in their first years. That demand adds to the remote workers, families and retirees who already make up most of the city's foreign renters.

Should you sign a lease now or wait for 2027?

If you are moving within a year, sign a 12 month lease and fix the renewal terms now. A clause that caps the rent increase at renewal, for example at 5 percent, protects you from whatever the market does in 2027. Ask for it before you sign, since landlords rarely add it later.

Time your search, too. October to February is the busiest season for long-term rentals in Da Nang, and the best homes go first. Outside those months there is more choice and more room to negotiate. Our Son Tra and My An comparison helps you pick the area before you start viewing.

Frequently asked questions

How much is rent in Da Nang in 2026?

In Alice Rentals listings on 5 November 2026, a one bedroom apartment had a median rent of $688 a month across 30 homes, from $380, and a two bedroom $1,400 across 35 homes. Two or three bedroom houses had a median of $1,830. Leases run six months or more, and electricity is billed on top.

Which area of Da Nang has the cheapest rent?

For a one bedroom apartment, My An and Son Tra, with median rents of $645 and $646 a month in Alice Rentals listings on 5 November 2026. Ngu Hanh Son followed at $722. Hai Chau and the My Khe beachfront cost the most.

Are rents rising in Da Nang?

Slowly. Da Nang's statistics office reported rental prices up 2.03 percent in one month in early 2026. Apartment sale prices have risen faster, about 68 percent since early 2019 according to the Vietnam Association of Realtors, and new towers set the top of the rental market.

When will the Da Nang metro open?

Not before 2032. Groundbreaking is planned for February 2027 on a line of more than 103 km from Da Nang airport to Hoi An and on to Chu Lai airport, costing about $10.1 billion. The first phase, from the airport to Hoi An, has 14 stations.

Why is 2027 important for Da Nang?

Several large projects start or switch on: metro groundbreaking planned for February, six new aircraft stands at the airport in July, work on a larger terminal from August, and construction at the Lien Chieu port and the free trade zone. On 1 January 2027 the city also marks 30 years as a centrally governed city.

Should I sign a long lease in Da Nang now?

If you are moving within a year, yes. A 12 month lease with a cap on the renewal increase, for example 5 percent, protects you from price changes in 2027. October to February is the busiest rental season, so start looking early if you arrive then.
