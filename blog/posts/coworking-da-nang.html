Where to Work in Da Nang: Coworking Spaces and Laptop Cafes by Neighbourhood

The desks, the day passes, and the apartments ten minutes from them.

Which area is best for working in Da Nang?

Base yourself in An Thuong, My An or Khue My. These three areas sit side by side behind My Khe beach, and you can walk between most of the spaces in this guide in under fifteen minutes. The Han riverside works if you prefer city streets to beach streets, and Son Tra suits you if you want sea views from your desk.

Try the desks before you sign the lease. The space you actually work well in should decide the street you live on.

Where should you work in An Thuong and My An?

At ACE or HIVE. This is the centre of the remote work scene, and the area most new arrivals choose to live in.

ACE Coworking

12 Tran Van Du, My An. Around 180,000 ₫ a day, 3,000,000 ₫ a month. 7am to 2am, Sundays to 6:30pm.

ACE spreads over three floors: a cafe on the ground floor, hot desks with ergonomic chairs and free external monitors in the middle, and a rooftop terrace with phone booths for calls. The community is what sets it apart, with weekly beach pickleball, group lunches and a members' WhatsApp group. It stays open until 2am, which suits anyone working to American hours.

HIVE

48 An Thuong 5. Around 210,000 to 260,000 ₫ a day. 7:30am to midnight.

HIVE opened in June 2024 and splits into two rooms. The open plan Studio has eleven desks, some with monitors. The quiet Focus room has four desks, each with an electric standing desk, an ergonomic chair and dual monitors. There is also a juice bar, a rooftop garden and ice bath equipment. HIVE does not publish a fixed price list and rates change, so ask when you book.

If you like to live where you work, look at apartments within the An Thuong grid or along the streets running back from My An beach. The Son Tra and My An comparison explains how this area feels to live in day to day, including the noise.

Where should you work in Khue My?

At Coworking Da Nang or Hana's. Khue My sits south of My An, a few streets from Alice's own office, and it is quieter than An Thuong while keeping you walking distance from the beach.

Coworking Da Nang

28 Tran Van Thanh. Around 200,000 ₫ a day, 3,000,000 ₫ a month. 8am to 10:30pm, members 24/7.

Ergonomic chairs, fast internet and a garden you can work from. The owners run meetups such as group dinners, pool tournaments and bowling nights. Weekly and monthly members get 24 hour access, which makes it one of the better options for late calls.

Hana's Coworking

69 Bui Ta Han. Around 70,000 ₫ for three hours, 130,000 ₫ a day, 2,000,000 ₫ a month. 6am to 11pm daily.

The budget pick, with a community feel and regular workshops and meetups for members. The three hour pass is useful if you only need a desk for a stretch of calls rather than a full day.

Where should you work in Son Tra?

Pick Son Tra if you want the sea in your window rather than a walkable street outside it.

Seaview Coworking

32 Ha Chuong. Around 135,000 ₫ a day, 2,000,000 ₫ a month. Members 24/7.

A short walk from My Khe beach, with views along the coast and 24 hour access for members. One remote worker described the ninth floor setting as giving views of the mountains around the city on clear days.

Enosta Space

16 to 18 An Nhon 3. From about $4.70 a day or $82 a month. Weekdays 8am to 6:30pm.

Formerly Enouvo, this combines coworking and coliving in an eight storey building with private rooms and dorms upstairs. You get fixed and hot desks, meeting rooms, free coffee and tea, and monitor rental. It keeps office hours, so plan evening calls elsewhere.

Where should you work near the river and city centre?

At Circo or Nomads, and at the airport space only if you are between flights.

Circo

17 Quang Trung, Hai Chau. Around 150,000 ₫ a day, 1,800,000 ₫ a month. Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm.

Part of a Vietnamese coworking brand with six other locations in Ho Chi Minh City. At 1,800,000 ₫ a month it is the cheapest monthly desk in this guide, but it closes at 6pm and all day Sunday, so it only works if you keep local hours.

Nomads Coworking

57A Le Loi, Hai Chau. 79,000 ₫ trial day pass. 7am to 10pm.

Opened in January 2025 with ergonomic chairs, laptop stands and air conditioning. The cheap trial pass makes it an easy one to test first.

Coworking Space Da Nang Airport

212 Nguyen Tri Phuong, Thanh Khe. Around 280,000 ₫ a day. 8am to 11pm.

Five minutes' walk from the terminal, with lockers, a live flight board and short term baggage storage. It is the most expensive day pass here, so treat it as a layover option rather than a daily desk.

Which cafes can you work from?

More than any list can hold, but a few names come up again and again among remote workers here.

  • Gozar Coffee, An Thuong. Has a dedicated quiet floor and suits a full working day.
  • I vegan, An Thuong. Gives diners free use of a coworking space on its third floor.
  • The Hideout, 24 K72 Nguyen Van Thoai. Hidden in a quiet alley, strong coffee and good breakfasts, about fifteen minutes' walk from My Khe beach.
  • The Cups, several branches. Proper air conditioning and clean bathrooms, though the music can run loud, so bring headphones.

Beach clubs on My An look tempting but make poor offices. One reviewer measured 5 Mbps and found no power outlets at all.

The local convention for staying welcome in a cafe is simple: order something every 90 minutes to two hours.

What should you check before renting an apartment to work from?

Five things, and the internet is only the first of them. Your apartment becomes your main office on rainy days, early calls and late calls, so it is worth being fussy before you sign rather than after.

  • The internet provider and speed. Ask which provider the building uses and run a speed test on your phone during the viewing. Buildings differ a lot, even on the same street.
  • Where the router sits. A router in the living room can leave a bedroom desk with a weak signal. Ask whether the landlord will add a second access point or run a cable.
  • A real desk and chair. Landlords will often add a desk if you ask before signing, and far less often after.
  • Noise. Visit on a weekday morning. Construction and karaoke both carry in Da Nang, and a quiet Sunday viewing tells you very little.
  • Power cuts in storm season. From September to December storms can cut power for hours. Keep mobile data as a backup and ask whether the building has a generator for the lifts and common areas.

Alice checks these on every viewing for remote workers, and asks landlords for changes before the lease reaches you.

How do time zones work from Da Nang?

Vietnam runs on UTC+7 all year with no daylight saving, so the offsets never shift under you. In practice:

  • Europe. Five to seven hours behind you. Your afternoon covers their morning and you finish by 11pm.
  • US East Coast. Eleven to twelve hours behind. A 9am New York meeting lands at 8 or 9pm in Da Nang.
  • US West Coast. Fourteen to fifteen hours behind, so their afternoon falls in your early morning.
  • Australia. Sydney runs three to four hours ahead, which gives you an easy overlap.

If your team sits in the Americas, choose ACE, HIVE or a space with 24 hour member access, and rent within walking distance so a late call ends with a five minute walk home rather than a ride.

Pick the space for your calendar, then pick the apartment for the walk home from it.

When is Da Nang busiest for remote workers?

From mid January to the end of April, when many people leave Chiang Mai for its burning season. Cafes and coworking spaces fill up and the social calendar gets fuller.

Apartments near An Thuong also go faster in that window, so book a month or two ahead if you plan to arrive in it. The month by month guide covers the weather across the rest of the year.

Frequently asked questions

How much does coworking cost in Da Nang?

Day passes cost roughly 80,000 to 280,000 dong, about $3 to $11. Monthly hot desks run roughly 1,800,000 to 5,000,000 dong, about $70 to $190. Rates move, and several spaces do not publish a fixed price list, so confirm directly before travelling.

What is the best coworking space in Da Nang?

ACE Coworking in My An has the strongest community and stays open late. Coworking Da Nang in Khue My offers 24 hour access for members. Hana's Coworking is the best value of the three.

Which area of Da Nang is best for digital nomads?

An Thuong, My An and Khue My. They sit side by side behind My Khe beach and hold most of the city's coworking spaces and laptop friendly cafes, close enough to walk between in about fifteen minutes.

Is the internet fast in Da Nang?

Coworking spaces run fibre and some offer wired connections above 800 Mbps. Apartment internet varies a lot by building, even on the same street, so test it during the viewing and ask which provider the building uses.

Can I work from cafes in Da Nang?

Yes. Cafes such as Gozar Coffee, The Hideout and The Cups welcome laptops. The local convention is to order something every 90 minutes to two hours to stay welcome.

Which coworking spaces in Da Nang are open late?

ACE Coworking opens until 2am Monday to Saturday and HIVE until midnight, while Coworking Da Nang and Seaview Coworking give members 24 hour access. These are the ones to choose if you take calls with the Americas.
