Should You Rent a House or a Villa in Da Nang?

Prices from 26 live listings, where the villas are, and what to check before you sign.

Should you rent a house or an apartment in Da Nang?

Rent a house or villa if you want space, a garden or a pool, or room for children and guests. Rent an apartment if you want a gym, a pool you don't maintain and a building that handles repairs. In Da Nang a house often costs less than an apartment with the same number of bedrooms, but you take on more of the upkeep.

Houses and villas made up 26 of my 97 homes for rent on 15 November 2026. The rest were apartments, so there is less to choose from, and the right house can take longer to find.

In Da Nang a three bedroom house often costs less than a three bedroom apartment.

How much does it cost to rent a house or villa in Da Nang?

A house costs about $1,400 a month and a villa about $3,600. Those were the medians across my 26 houses and villas on 15 November 2026, for leases of six months or more. The range runs from $950 for a three bedroom house in Son Tra to $11,000 for a beachfront villa.

Is a house cheaper than an apartment in Da Nang?

For three bedrooms or more, usually yes. In my listings on 15 November 2026, a three bedroom house or villa had a median rent of $1,740 across 9 homes. A three bedroom apartment had a median of $2,500 across 5. The houses were also bigger: their listed sizes had a median of 300 m², against 120 m² for the apartments, though some villa listings give the plot rather than the floor area.

The apartment price pays for the building: a lobby, a lift, a shared pool and gym, and often a sea or river view from a high floor. With a house you get the space and do without the shared facilities. For one or two people, an apartment stays cheaper. What is the difference between a house and a villa in Da Nang?

A house in Da Nang usually means a townhouse, called nhà phố in Vietnamese. It is narrow and tall, with two to four floors, shares its side walls with the neighbours and opens straight onto a street or an alley. A villa, or biệt thự, stands on its own plot or in a gated community, usually with a garden and sometimes a pool.

The words overlap in listings. Some owners call a wide three storey townhouse a villa, so look at the plot and the floor plan, not the name. In my listings, the five houses ran from 125 to 300 m², and the villas from 100 m² to plots of about 1,000 m².

Where are the houses and villas in Da Nang?

Mostly in Ngu Hanh Son, the southern beach district on the road to Hoi An. 16 of my 26 houses and villas were there on 15 November 2026, many of them inside gated communities. Hai Chau, Son Tra and Cam Le have smaller houses and villas in ordinary streets, closer to the centre and for less money.

In Ngu Hanh Son, the villas I list sit in a handful of communities. Fusion Resort & Villas and The Ocean Villas are resort compounds on the beach. Regal Victoria, FPT City and the Nam Viet A streets near the river are residential, with more space for the money. One River has newer villas by the river.

In My An and on the My Khe beachfront I had no houses listed that day. Those areas are mostly apartments, which is why families who want a house often look further south. How much is a villa with a pool in Da Nang?

From about $2,100 a month. On 15 November 2026, 8 of my 26 house and villa listings mentioned a pool. The cheapest was a four bedroom villa in Nam Viet A at $2,105, followed by a new four bedroom villa in FPT City at $2,320. At the top, a six bedroom pool villa at One River was $7,485. The beachfront villas at Fusion Resort & Villas and The Ocean Villas, from $3,600 to $11,000, sit in resort grounds by the beach.

A pool brings running costs. The pump runs for hours every day, and the water needs cleaning and chemicals every week. Ask whether the rent includes pool and garden care, and write the answer into the contract. In the resort compounds, ask what the monthly fee covers, such as security, shared gardens and beach access.

What does it cost to run a house in Da Nang?

More than an apartment, mainly because of electricity. A house usually has an air conditioner in each bedroom, often a water heater in each bathroom, and sometimes a pool pump, so the bill grows with the size of the home. Ask how the landlord bills it. Some pass on the Vietnam Electricity bill as it comes, at the household tariff of about 1,984 to 3,460 dong per kWh, and some charge a fixed rate of 4,000 to 6,000 dong. Get the rate in writing.

Other costs are smaller. Water is a flat fee or billed by the cubic metre, rubbish collection costs 30,000 to 50,000 dong a month, and parking is free at your own gate, where an apartment building charges for a motorbike space. Our guide to renting in Da Nang as a foreigner lists the running costs line by line, with deposits and police registration.

What should you check before renting a house in Da Nang?

Flooding, stairs, water and repairs. An apartment building deals with most of these for you. In a house they are yours to ask about before you sign.

  • Flooding. Ask whether the street floods in October and November, and look for a water line low on the walls. Ground floors take the water first, so check where the bedrooms are.
  • Stairs. A townhouse spreads over three or four floors, usually with the kitchen at the bottom and the bedrooms at the top. Think about small children and older parents. Only some villas have a lift.
  • Water. Many houses pump water to a tank on the roof. Run the shower on the top floor and check the hot water in every bathroom.
  • Repairs. Agree who services the air conditioners, deals with pests, and fixes the pump and the roof, and put it in the contract.
  • Security. A house has no reception desk. Check the gate, the locks and the window grilles, and ask whether a gated community has a guard at night.

The rainy season runs from about September to December. If you view in the dry months, ask the neighbours too. Our weather guide shows what each month is like.

When should you start looking for a house in Da Nang?

Earlier than for an apartment, because there are fewer of them. Houses and villas were about a quarter of my listings on 15 November 2026, and a family with fixed needs, such as four bedrooms near a school or a pool, may have only a few to choose from at any time.

Send your budget, bedrooms and move-in date a few weeks before you arrive, and plan a day of viewings in your first week. October to February is the busiest season for long leases in Da Nang, when the best homes go first. If schools matter, our guide to living in Da Nang with kids shows which areas suit the school run.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a house in Da Nang?

In Alice Rentals listings on 15 November 2026, a house had a median rent of $1,370 a month across 5 homes, from $950 for three bedrooms in Son Tra. A villa had a median of $3,600 across 21 homes, from $1,520 to $11,000. Leases run six months or more, and electricity is billed on top.

Is it cheaper to rent a house or an apartment in Da Nang?

For three bedrooms or more, a house is usually cheaper. In Alice Rentals listings on 15 November 2026, a three bedroom house or villa had a median rent of $1,740 a month, against $2,500 for a three bedroom apartment, and the houses had about twice the listed space. For one or two bedrooms, an apartment is cheaper.

Where can I rent a villa in Da Nang?

Mostly in Ngu Hanh Son, the southern beach district, which had 16 of Alice Rentals' 26 houses and villas on 15 November 2026. Communities there include Fusion Resort & Villas, The Ocean Villas, Regal Victoria, FPT City and Nam Viet A. Hai Chau and Son Tra have smaller houses and villas in town.

How much is a villa with a private pool in Da Nang?

From about $2,100 a month. On 15 November 2026 the cheapest pool villa in Alice Rentals listings was four bedrooms in Nam Viet A at $2,105. A six bedroom pool villa at One River was $7,485, and beachfront villas at Fusion Resort & Villas and The Ocean Villas ran from $3,600 to $11,000. Ask whether the rent includes pool and garden care.

What is the difference between a house and a villa in Vietnam?

A house, or nhà phố, is usually a narrow townhouse of two to four floors that shares its side walls and opens onto a street or alley. A villa, or biệt thự, stands on its own plot or in a gated community, usually with a garden and sometimes a pool. Listings use the words loosely, so check the plot and floor plan.

What should I check before renting a house in Da Nang?

Check whether the street floods in October and November, how many stairs there are, the water pressure and hot water on the top floor, who pays for repairs, pool and garden care, and how electricity is billed. Put the answers in the contract.
