What Are the Best Coffee Shops in Da Nang?

Five cafes worth crossing town for, what each does best, and what to order.

By Alice Pham, Private Rental Advisor in Đà Nẵng · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · 6 min read

What are the best coffee shops in Da Nang?

XLIII Specialty Coffee for serious coffee, Cong Ca Phe for coconut coffee by the river, Trinh Cafe for a local favourite, Wonderlust for coffee with cake in the city centre, and The Hideout for a quiet morning of work near the beach. Da Nang has thousands of cafes, and these five each do one thing better than the rest.

  1. XLIII Specialty Coffee: beans roasted on site, near My An beach. Best for specialty coffee.
  2. Cong Ca Phe, Bach Dang: coconut coffee on the Han River. Best for a classic Vietnamese cafe.
  3. Trinh Cafe: the local brand, known for avocado coffee. Best for local prices.
  4. Wonderlust Bakery & Coffee: coffee with cakes in the city centre. Best for a slow break.
  5. The Hideout: a quiet alley cafe in An Thuong. Best for working.

Five cafes, five reasons to cross town. Start with coconut coffee by the river.

1. XLIII Specialty Coffee: the best specialty coffee in Da Nang

At XLIII Specialty Coffee, a few streets back from My An beach in Ngu Hanh Son. It began as 43 Factory Coffee Roaster, and older maps and reviews still use that name. The beans are roasted in the same room you drink them in, and the menu covers espresso drinks and hand brews.

It opens at 6:30am, which makes it an easy first stop after a morning swim. It is also one of the cafes remote workers return to most, so come early if you want a table near a socket.

2. Cong Ca Phe: where to get coconut coffee in Da Nang

At Cong Ca Phe on Bach Dang street, facing the Han River. Cong is a Vietnamese chain with a retro look in army green, and its coconut coffee is the drink people come for: strong coffee over a frozen coconut cream, somewhere between a coffee and a dessert.

The branch at 98-96 Bach Dang opens from 7am to 11:30pm. Take the stairs and ask for a seat on the second floor, which has the best view of the river. It is a short walk from the Dragon Bridge, so it works well before the weekend fire show at 9pm.

3. Trinh Cafe: where locals drink coffee in Da Nang

At Trinh Cafe, a Da Nang brand with several branches across Hai Chau, including 25 Pham Hong Thai near Han Market and a kiosk on the Bach Dang walking street. It is best known for avocado coffee, which blends avocado with coffee and condensed milk into something thick and sweet that tastes better than it sounds.

Prices run from about 40,000 to 100,000 dong a drink, less than in the foreigner cafes by the beach.

4. Wonderlust Bakery & Coffee: the best cafe for coffee and cake

Wonderlust Bakery & Coffee at 96 Tran Phu, in the city centre a few streets from the river. It pairs its coffee with cakes and pastries, and it suits a slow morning or an afternoon break from the heat.

It opens from 9am to 10pm, and the riverside walk is a few streets east.

5. The Hideout: the best coffee shop in Da Nang for working

The Hideout, at 24 K72 Nguyen Van Thoai in An Thuong, hidden down a quiet alley about fifteen minutes' walk from My Khe beach. It serves strong coffee and good breakfasts, and the alley keeps the traffic noise out.

Wherever you work, stay welcome by ordering something every 90 minutes to two hours, which is the local convention. Beach clubs look tempting but make poor offices, with slow wifi and few power outlets. Our coworking guide lists the spaces with proper desks and day passes.

What should you order in a Da Nang coffee shop?

Start with cà phê sữa đá, strong coffee dripped through a small metal filter over sweet condensed milk and ice. It is what most of the city drinks. After that:

  • Coconut coffee (cà phê cốt dừa): coffee over frozen coconut cream. Best at Cong.
  • Salt coffee (cà phê muối): coffee topped with lightly salted cream, a drink from Hue that is now on menus across Da Nang.
  • Avocado coffee (cà phê bơ): a thick, sweet blend of avocado and coffee. Best at Trinh.
  • Bạc xỉu: mostly milk with a little coffee, for an afternoon when you want something gentler.

Vietnamese coffee is strong and usually made from robusta beans. If you want less sugar, ask for ít đường.

Which area of Da Nang has the best cafes?

An Thuong and My An for cafes to work from, and Hai Chau for local coffee culture. An Thuong's grid of streets behind the beach has the densest mix of foreigner friendly cafes, coworking spaces and breakfast spots, all within walking distance. Hai Chau, across the river, has the older local cafes, the riverside and Han Market.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best coffee shops in Da Nang?

XLIII Specialty Coffee for specialty coffee, Cong Ca Phe on Bach Dang for coconut coffee by the river, Trinh Cafe for local avocado coffee, Wonderlust Bakery & Coffee for coffee and cake, and The Hideout in An Thuong for a quiet place to work.

Is 43 Factory Coffee still open in Da Nang?

Yes, under a new name. 43 Factory Coffee Roaster became XLIII Specialty Coffee, at Lot 422 Ngo Thi Sy near My An beach in Ngu Hanh Son. It roasts its beans on site and opens from 6:30am to 10:30pm.

Where can I get coconut coffee in Da Nang?

At Cong Ca Phe, 98-96 Bach Dang, facing the Han River. It is open from 7am to 11:30pm, and the second floor has the best river view. Coconut coffee is strong coffee served over frozen coconut cream.

What is avocado coffee?

A thick, sweet drink made by blending avocado with coffee and condensed milk. In Da Nang it is the signature drink of Trinh Cafe, a local brand with branches in Hai Chau, where drinks cost about 40,000 to 100,000 dong.

Which Da Nang cafe is best for working on a laptop?

The Hideout in An Thuong is quiet and serves strong coffee and breakfast, and XLIII opens at 6:30am with space for laptops. Order something every 90 minutes to two hours to stay welcome, and avoid beach clubs, which often have slow wifi and few outlets.

What coffee should I order in Vietnam?

Start with cà phê sữa đá, strong drip coffee over condensed milk and ice. Then try coconut coffee, salt coffee and avocado coffee. Ask for ít đường if you want less sugar.
