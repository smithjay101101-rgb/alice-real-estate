Before you buy

Questions buyers ask Alice

Can foreigners buy property in Đà Nẵng?

Yes, with limits. Foreigners can own apartments and houses in approved residential projects for up to 50 years, with the option to extend. Foreign buyers can hold up to 30% of the apartments in one building and up to 250 houses in one ward. Foreigners cannot own land outright. The rules do change, so check the current position with a lawyer before you pay a deposit.

Which areas of Đà Nẵng can Alice search?

Anywhere in the city: the beach side around Mỹ Khê, Sơn Trà and Mỹ An, Ngũ Hành Sơn further south, the centre in Hải Châu, and quieter Cẩm Lệ. If you are still choosing an area, the guide to Sơn Trà vs Mỹ An and the rentals by area are a good place to start.
