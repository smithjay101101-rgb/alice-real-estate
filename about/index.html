About

Who is Alice, the rental agent in Da Nang?

Alice Pham was born in Da Nang, has lived here her whole life, and has spent since 2019 helping foreign renters find somewhere to live in the city she grew up in. More than 500 families have moved into a home she found for them.

Who is Alice Pham?

Alice Pham is a licensed rental agent working across Da Nang. She was born in the city and has lived here her entire life, which is the part that matters when you are choosing between two streets you have never seen.

Growing up here means she knows things that do not appear on a listing. Which alleys in An Thuong hold water after a hard October rain. Which buildings put their generator under the bedrooms. Which landlords answer the phone in year two of a lease, and which ones stop answering the day the deposit clears.

She works in Vietnamese and English, so contracts, landlord negotiations and police registration all happen in the language they need to happen in, and nothing gets agreed that you have not read.

How did she end up doing this?

It started with friends of friends. Foreigners arriving in Da Nang kept getting quoted prices no local would pay, signing contracts they could not read, and finding out after moving in that the building had no permit to house them.

Alice started sitting in on those viewings and reading those contracts, and by 2019 it was the job. She took the licence, and the work settled into a simple rule she still uses: tell people the truth about a property even when the truth loses the deal.

That rule is why she will talk a client out of a place that photographs well and sits above a karaoke bar, and why a good share of her clients arrive through someone she placed two years earlier.

What does she actually do for you?

The short version is that she does the parts that are hard to do from outside Vietnam, or in a language you do not read. You tell her a budget, an area and a move-in date, and she comes back with places that fit rather than a link dump.

  • A shortlist built from properties she has seen in person, not reposted listings that were taken last month.
  • Viewings arranged in one run, so you see five or six places in an afternoon instead of over a week.
  • Negotiation with the landlord in Vietnamese, on rent, on the electricity rate, on furniture and on the length of the term.
  • A contract read line by line before you sign, with anything unusual translated and explained.
  • Handover support, including meter readings, the inventory and the temporary residence registration the landlord is responsible for filing.
  • Someone to call in month seven when the air conditioning fails and the landlord is not answering.

If a place is wrong for you, she will say so. That is the whole reason clients send their friends.

Questions about working with Alice

How does Alice Rentals work?

You send your budget, preferred area and move-in date, and Alice shortlists properties that match and arranges the viewings. She handles the negotiation with the landlord in Vietnamese, reviews the contract with you in English, and confirms your police registration is completed after you move in.

Do landlords in Da Nang speak English?

Some do, most do not. Outside the buildings that market specifically to foreigners, viewings and negotiations usually happen in Vietnamese. This is the main practical reason foreigners work with an agent here rather than the price of the apartment itself.

What is the best time of year to rent in Da Nang?

Rates are softest from roughly May to August, when tourist demand shifts to short stays and long term inventory sits empty. October through February is the busiest stretch for long term rentals as people arrive to escape northern winters, and prices firm up accordingly.
